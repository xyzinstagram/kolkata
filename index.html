Comprehensive documentation of Kolkata (Calcutta) urban history. Expert coverage of the Howrah Bridge, Victoria Memorial, Maidan, and Prinsep Ghat. Specialized in the cinematic aesthetics of North Kolkata, Yellow Taxis, Vintage Trams, and the Kolkata Metro (including underwater lines).
